# Knowledge Module 01 — Short-Stay Visas

## Purpose

This module contains validated information used by the AI assistant to answer customer enquiries relating to short stays in France and the Schengen area.

## Key Concepts

A short-stay visa generally concerns stays of up to 90 days within a 180-day period.

Typical purposes may include:

- Tourism
- Business travel
- Family or private visits
- Short training programmes
- Short educational programmes

## Important Distinction

Visa validity and authorised duration of stay are not the same thing.

A multiple-entry visa may remain valid for a longer period while still limiting the total permitted stay according to applicable Schengen rules.

## Customer Service Logic

Before providing an answer, determine:

1. Applicant nationality
2. Country of legal residence
3. Main destination
4. Purpose of travel
5. Intended duration
6. Existing visa or residence permit, where relevant

If this information is insufficient, the assistant should ask clarification questions rather than assume the appropriate visa category.

## Source

Official France-Visas and European Union immigration/visa information should be used as authoritative sources.
