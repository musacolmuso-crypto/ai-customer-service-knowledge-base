# Knowledge Module 03 — Family Members of French and EU Citizens

## Purpose

This module helps the AI identify family-related visa enquiries.

## Key Principle

The statement:

"I'm married to a European citizen"

does not provide enough information to determine the correct procedure.

## Required Clarification

The assistant should establish:

1. Applicant nationality
2. Spouse's nationality
3. Destination country
4. Country where the EU citizen resides
5. Whether the applicant is travelling with or joining the EU citizen
6. Length of intended stay
7. Nature of the legally recognised relationship

## Example

### Customer

I am South African and married to an Italian citizen. What visa do I need to travel to France?

### Expected AI Behaviour

Do not immediately assign a visa category.

Establish whether the Italian spouse resides in France, whether the applicant is travelling with or joining the spouse, the intended duration of stay, and other relevant circumstances.

## AI Safety Rule

When several legal pathways could apply, the assistant should request clarification rather than infer the applicant's immigration status.
