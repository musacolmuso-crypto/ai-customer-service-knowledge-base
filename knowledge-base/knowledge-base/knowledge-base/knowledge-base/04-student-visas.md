# Knowledge Module 04 — Student Visas

## Purpose

Support customer enquiries relating to study in France.

## Information Required

The assistant should establish:

1. Applicant nationality
2. Country of residence
3. Duration of studies
4. Type of educational programme
5. Admission status
6. Relevant student procedure
7. Intended arrival date

## Example

### Customer

I have been accepted for a two-year Master's programme in France.

### AI Reasoning

The duration immediately indicates that this should not be treated as an ordinary short-stay tourism enquiry.

The assistant should retrieve the relevant long-stay student procedure from the validated sources.

## Ambiguous Example

### Customer

I'm studying in Paris. What visa do I need?

### Correct Behaviour

Ask how long the course lasts before determining the visa category.
