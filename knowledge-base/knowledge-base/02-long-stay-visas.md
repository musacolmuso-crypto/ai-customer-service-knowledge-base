# Knowledge Module 02 — Long-Stay Visas

## Purpose

This module supports enquiries concerning stays in France exceeding the normal short-stay period.

## Typical Long-Stay Scenarios

These may include:

- University studies
- Employment
- Family reunification
- Joining a spouse
- Long-term residence
- Certain professional activities

## Customer Service Logic

The assistant should establish:

1. Intended duration of stay
2. Purpose of stay
3. Applicant nationality
4. Country of legal residence
5. Family relationship, where applicable
6. Study or employment circumstances, where applicable

## Important Rule

The assistant must not assume that a long-validity multiple-entry Schengen visa automatically permits continuous residence in France.

## Escalation

Cases involving unusual immigration status, conflicting documentation or circumstances not covered by the validated knowledge base should be escalated rather than answered speculatively.
